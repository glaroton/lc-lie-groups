# lc-lie-groups

By [@glaroton](https://github.com/glaroton).

**Lie groups modeled on locally convex spaces, formalized in Lean 4 and Mathlib.**

API documentation (generated by doc-gen4): <https://glaroton.github.io/lc-lie-groups/docs/>
Blueprint: <https://glaroton.github.io/lc-lie-groups/blueprint/>
(PDF: <https://glaroton.github.io/lc-lie-groups/blueprint.pdf>)

> **Status.** Early stage: Lie groups, their Lie algebras and the Lie functor, and `Diff(K)` for
> compact `K` as a Lie group. The library builds with no `sorry`, and its main theorems depend
> only on Lean's standard axioms. It is the third layer of a program on infinite-dimensional Lie
> theory: [lc-analysis](https://github.com/glaroton/lc-analysis) provides the Michal–Bastiani
> calculus, and [lc-manifolds](https://github.com/glaroton/lc-manifolds) the manifolds, the
> manifolds of mappings `C^ℓ(M, N)` and the composition map, and the fact that `Diff_{C^ℓ}(K)` is
> open in `C^ℓ(K, K)` for a compact manifold `K`.

## Convention: model spaces need not be complete

As in the companion libraries, model spaces are Hausdorff locally convex spaces, **not assumed
complete**, following Glöckner–Neeb [GN26, §5.7]. Results that need completeness (exponential
maps, regularity, integration of Lie algebra homomorphisms) state it as a hypothesis.

## Contents

### `LieGroup/Basic.lean`

* `MBLieGroup E G`: a manifold `G` modeled on the locally convex space `E`, with a group
  structure whose multiplication and inversion are smooth [GN26, Definition 5.1.1(b)].
* `MBLieGroup.smoothMBM_mul_left`, `smoothMBM_mul_right`, `smoothMBM_conj`: left and right
  multiplications and conjugations are smooth.
* `MBLieGroup.isTopologicalGroup`: a Lie group is a topological group.
* `MBLieGroup.IsHom`: morphisms of Lie groups, i.e. smooth homomorphisms
  [GN26, Definition 5.1.1(c)]; `IsHom.id`, `IsHom.comp`.

### `LieGroup/OfVaryingAtlas.lean`

* `IsVaryingLieGroup A`: the group operations of `G` are smooth for an atlas with varying models
  `A` (the kind of atlas that groups of mappings carry).
* `IsVaryingLieGroup.chartedSpace`, `IsVaryingLieGroup.hasGroupoid`, `IsVaryingLieGroup.mbLieGroup`:
  the translates `x ↦ φ(g⁻¹ x)` of the chart `φ` at the identity form an atlas modeled on a single
  space, and `G` is then a Lie group (the charts of the proof of [GN26, Theorem 5.3.1]).

### `LieGroup/OpenSubgroup.lean`

* `smoothMBM_subtype_val`, `smoothMBM_of_val_comp`: smooth maps and open subsets of manifolds.
* `OpenSubgroup.instMBLieGroup`: **open subgroups of Lie groups are Lie groups**;
  `OpenSubgroup.lieEquiv`: `L(U) ≅ L(G)`.
* `MBLieGroup.identityComponent`: the identity component `G₀` is an open subgroup, since Lie
  groups are locally connected (`locallyConnectedSpace_of_locallyConvex`).

### `LieGroup/Vector.lean` and `LieGroup/Prod.lean`

* `VectorGroup.instMBLieGroup`: **vector groups** `(E, +)` (as `Multiplicative E`, with the
  global chart `toAdd`) are Lie groups [GN26, Example 5.1.2]; `VectorGroup.lie_eq_zero`: their
  Lie algebras are abelian [GN26, Example 5.1.9(a)].
* `MBLieGroup.instProd`: **products of Lie groups are Lie groups**, modeled on `E × F`
  (lc-manifolds' product manifolds).
* `LocalGroupLaw.prod`, `LocalGroupLaw.bracket_prod`: products of local group laws have
  componentwise brackets; `MBLieGroup.LieAlg.prodEquiv`: **`L(G × H) ≅ L(G) × L(H)`** as Lie
  algebras.

### `LieGroup/Units.lean`

* `UnitGroup A`, `UnitGroup.instMBLieGroup`: **the unit group of a continuous inverse algebra is a
  Lie group** [GN26, Example 5.1.4], with the global chart `x ↦ x − 1` [GN26, Example 5.1.9(b)].
  Inversion is smooth by lc-analysis's `smoothMBOn_inverse` [GN26, Corollary 1.3.8].
  `UnitGroup A` is a type synonym for `Aˣ`, since Mathlib's charted space on the units of a
  complete normed ring uses the chart `u ↦ u`.
* `quasiLaw A`: the group law `x ∗ y = x + y + xy` on a (possibly non-unital) locally convex
  algebra [GN26, Definition 5.1.3(b)]; `quasiLaw_b2`, `quasiLaw_bracket`: `b₂(x, y) = xy` and
  `[x, y] = xy − yx`.
* `UnitGroup.lie_eq`: **`L(A^×)` is `A` with the commutator bracket** [GN26, Example 5.1.9(b)],
  using `LocalGroupLaw.bracket_congr` (the bracket only depends on the multiplication near the
  identity).

### `LieAlgebra/Local.lean`

* `LocalGroupLaw E`: a smooth local group law on `E`, such as the multiplication of a Lie group
  in a chart at the identity [GN26, Remark 5.1.8].
* `LocalGroupLaw.xl`, `LocalGroupLaw.xl_mul`: the local left invariant vector fields
  `x̃_l(z) = d(b ↦ z ∗ b)(1) x` [GN26, (5.4)], and their left invariance.
* `LocalGroupLaw.bracket`: the bracket `[x, y] = [x̃_l, ỹ_l](1)` [GN26, (5.5)];
  `localBracket_xl`: `[x̃_l, ỹ_l] = [x, y]~_l` near the identity [GN26, (5.3)];
  `bracket_jacobi`, `continuous_bracket` and bilinearity.
* `LocalGroupLaw.dMB_bracket_of_hom`: derivatives of local homomorphisms preserve brackets
  [GN26, Proposition 5.1.10]; `bracket_eq_zero_of_comm`: commutative laws have zero bracket
  [GN26, Example 5.1.9(a)].

### `LieAlgebra/Basic.lean`

* `MBLieGroup.LieAlg E G`: **the Lie algebra `L(G)`** [GN26, Definition 5.1.6], the model space
  `E ≅ T_1 G` with the bracket of left invariant vector fields computed in the chart at the
  identity; instances `LieRing` and `LieAlgebra ℝ`, and `LieAlg.continuous_lie`.
* `MBLieGroup.IsHom.lieHom`: `L(φ) = T_1(φ)` is a continuous Lie algebra homomorphism
  [GN26, Proposition 5.1.10]; `lieHom_id`, `lieHom_comp`: the Lie functor
  [GN26, Corollary 5.1.11].
* `MBLieGroup.LieAlg.lie_eq_zero_of_comm`: abelian Lie groups have abelian Lie algebras
  [GN26, Example 5.1.9(a)].

The proofs never use the tangent bundle of `G`: left translations relate the local fields
`x̃_l` to themselves, and lc-manifolds' transformation rule and Jacobi identity for local
brackets (`localBracket_transition`, `localBracket_jacobi`) do the rest.

### `LieAlgebra/LeftInvariant.lean`

* `MBLieGroup.leftField E x`: the left invariant vector field `x_l(g) = T_1(λ_g) x`
  [GN26, Definition 5.1.6] (`leftField_eq_tangentMap`, `leftField_one`), left invariant
  (`tangentMap_leftField`, [GN26, (5.2)]).
* `MBLieGroup.lieBracket_leftField`: **`[x_l, y_l] = [x, y]_l`** for lc-manifolds' bracket of
  vector fields [GN26, (5.3)], so the bracket of `L(G)` is the bracket of left invariant vector
  fields at the identity (`lieBracket_leftField_one`). In the chart at `g`, the left translation
  by `g` relates the fields `x̃_l` of the chart at the identity to `x_l`, which is the related
  vector field lemma [GN26, Lemma 3.4.17] in the form needed.

### `LieAlgebra/Taylor.lean`

* `LocalGroupLaw.b2`: the second order term `b₂(x, y) = dỹ_l(1) x` of the multiplication;
  `dMB_mul_one`, `dMB_dMB_mul_one`: `dm(1, 1)(x, y) = x + y` and
  `d²m(1, 1)((x, y), (x, y)) = 2 b₂(x, y)`; `b2_eq_mixed`, `continuous_b2`; and
  `bracket_eq_b2_sub_b2`: the bracket is the skew-symmetric part of `b₂`
  [GN26, Remark 5.1.8(a), Lemma 5.1.7].
* `LocalGroupLaw.LocalInverse`: a `C²` inversion `ι` near the identity. `dMB_inv_one`,
  `dMB_dMB_inv_one`: `dι(1) x = −x` and `d²ι(1)(x, x) = 2 b₂(x, x)` [GN26, (5.6)];
  `dMB_dMB_conj_one`, `dMB_dMB_commutator_one`: the conjugation `(x ∗ y) ∗ x⁻¹` and the
  commutator `x ∗ y ∗ x⁻¹ ∗ y⁻¹` have second order terms `y + [x, y]` and `[x, y]`
  [GN26, (5.7), (5.8)]. All from the second-order chain rule (lc-analysis, `dMB_dMB_comp`).
* `MBLieGroup.localInverse`, `MBLieGroup.LieAlg.dMB_dMB_commutator`: the inversion of `G` in the
  chart at the identity, and the commutator of `G` to second order.

### `Diffeomorphism/Basic.lean` and `Diffeomorphism/LieAlgebra.lean`

* `diffeos H K`, `diffeoAtlas A`: `Diff(K)` as an open subset of `C^∞(K, K)`, with the restricted
  manifold structure (`K` compact, `A` a local addition on `K`).
* `smooth_inv`: the inversion `Diff(K) → C^∞(K, K)`, `γ ↦ γ⁻¹`, is smooth
  [GN26, Proposition 13.1.10(a)]. By the exponential law this is [GN26, Lemma 13.1.8] for the
  evaluation map (lc-manifolds, `ContMDiffMB₂.inverse`), which rests on an implicit function
  theorem with locally convex parameters and finite-dimensional values (lc-analysis,
  `contDiffMBOn_of_implicit`).
* `isVaryingLieGroup_diffeos`: composition and inversion are smooth on `Diff(K)`.
* `Diffeo A`, `Diffeo.instMBLieGroup`: **`Diff(K)` is a Lie group**
  [GN26, Proposition 13.1.10(e)], modeled on `DiffeoModel A`, the smooth sections of `TK` along
  the identity (vector fields on `K`, in fiber coordinates).
* `Diffeo.toField_lie`: **`L(Diff(K))` is `V(K)` with the opposite bracket**
  [GN26, Example 5.4.3]: for a normalized local addition, `[x, y] = [Y, X]` for the vector fields
  `X`, `Y` with fiber coordinates `x`, `y` (lc-manifolds' `lieBracket`). Evaluation at a point
  commutes with the derivatives, so the bracket is a mixed second derivative of
  `(s, t) ↦ (s x ∗ t y)(p)`, computed in a chart of `K` (`Diffeo.evalAt_dMB_xl`); the
  normalization makes the second derivative of the local addition drop out.
* `UnitCircle`, `DiffCircle`, `DiffPlusCircle` (`Diffeomorphism/Circle.lean`): **`Diff(S¹)` and
  its identity component `Diff⁺(S¹)` are Lie groups** [GN26, §13.3], with `S¹ ⊆ ℝ²` and the
  normalized local addition of the sphere (lc-manifolds, `sphereLocalAddition`);
  `DiffPlusCircle.toField_lie`: **`L(Diff⁺(S¹)) ≅ V(S¹)^op`**.

## Planned

1. Examples: the universal covering group of `Diff⁺(S¹)` with the global chart
   `u ↦ id + u` on `{u ∈ C^∞_per : u' > −1}` [GN26, §13.3] (lc-manifolds, `DiffCircle.Ω`), and
   `Diff⁺(S¹)` as its quotient by `ℤ`. For `Diff(K)`: the finite-order statements of
   [GN26, Proposition 13.1.10(a), (b)] (`Diff_{C^{ℓ+k}}(K) → Diff_{C^ℓ}(K)` is `C^k`), and the
   compatibility of the translated atlas with the restricted atlas of `C^∞(K, K)`.
2. Lie algebras: `L(Diff(K))` as a Lie algebra isomorphism with `V(K)^op`, once `V(K)` is a Lie
   algebra type in lc-manifolds.
3. Exponential functions [GN26, §5.6] and regular Lie groups [GN26, Ch. 6], with the
   completeness hypotheses they need.
4. Invariant metrics on Lie groups: left and right invariant Finsler metrics from a norm on the
   Lie algebra (the general Finsler theory is in lc-manifolds).

## Building

```
lake exe cache get
lake build
```

`lake exe cache get` downloads Mathlib's compiled files; lc-analysis and lc-manifolds are built
from source on the first `lake build`.
