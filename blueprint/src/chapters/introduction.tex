\chapter{Introduction}
\label{chap:introduction}

This blueprint describes the mathematics formalized in the Lean library
\href{https://github.com/glaroton/lc-lie-groups}{lc-lie-groups}: Lie groups modeled on locally
convex spaces. Every definition and theorem below is linked to its Lean counterpart. All the
statements are proved in Lean, with no \texttt{sorry}, and the main theorems depend only on the
standard axioms of Lean.

The library rests on two companion libraries: lc-analysis \cite{LCA}, which provides the
Michal--Bastiani calculus on locally convex spaces, and lc-manifolds \cite{LCM}, which provides
manifolds modeled on locally convex spaces, manifolds of mappings, and the fact that the
diffeomorphisms of a compact manifold $K$ form an open subset of $C^\ell(K, K)$. We follow
Glöckner and Neeb \cite{GN26} and Neeb \cite{Nee06}.

As in the companion libraries, \textbf{model spaces need not be complete}, following
\cite{GN26}. Whenever a result needs completeness, it is part of the statement.
